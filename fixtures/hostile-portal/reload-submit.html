ADVERSARIAL FIXTURE — the post-fill reload. This models an ASP.NET portal that preserves field values across a postback and submits the form during load. On a first visit the fields are empty and nothing happens. On a reload carrying values — which is what the page looks like after the extension has filled it — it submits them immediately, before a gesture-injected content script could exist.

That sequence is the one the Phase 5 note did not cover. "Nothing of ours had been filled" is true of a first load and false of a reload after a fill.
